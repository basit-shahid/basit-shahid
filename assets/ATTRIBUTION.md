The technology marks in `icons/` and the GitHub mark in `links/github.svg` are from [Devicon v2.16.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.16.0), distributed under the MIT license below. Each source is the matching `*-original.svg` in Devicon’s `icons/` directory. Source folder mappings: `cpp` → `cplusplus`, `html` → `html5`, `css` → `css3`, `idea` → `intellij`; all other names match their source folders.
